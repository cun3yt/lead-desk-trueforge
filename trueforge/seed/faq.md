## How long does setup take?

About a day for most fleets. Import your vehicles from a spreadsheet, connect telematics, and invite your team. Growth and Enterprise customers get help from their success manager.

## Do you have an API?

Yes. A REST API and webhooks are available on every plan.

## Is there a free trial?

Yes, 14 days on any plan, no credit card needed.

## Do drivers need a paid seat?

No. Drivers using the mobile inspection app are always free.

## Can I switch plans later?

Yes. Upgrade any time; the price difference is prorated. Downgrades take effect at your next renewal.

## Which languages does the app support?

English, Spanish and French.

## How is support handled?

Starter includes email support. Growth adds priority support with a named success manager. Enterprise adds a dedicated onboarding team.
