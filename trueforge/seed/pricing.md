Simple per-seat pricing. A seat is one fleet manager, technician or office user. Drivers using the inspection app are free.

## Starter: $10 per seat per month

For small fleets getting organized.

- Up to 25 seats
- Preventive maintenance schedules, work orders, driver inspections
- Slack and QuickBooks integrations
- Email support

## Growth: $25 per seat per month

For growing fleets that need their tools connected. **Best fit for teams of 25 to 500 seats.**

- Everything in Starter
- SAP S/4HANA and Salesforce integrations
- Parts inventory and cost reports
- Priority support with a named success manager

## Enterprise: custom pricing

For fleets with more than 500 seats or special requirements.

- Everything in Growth
- Single sign-on (SSO) and audit logs
- Custom contract and invoicing
- Dedicated onboarding team

All plans are billed annually. Monthly billing adds 20%. Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial.
