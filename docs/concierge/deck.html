Concierge

A website agent for every Ambiguous client, running on their own workspace.

Cuneyt & Oskardemo company: Acme · fleet maintenance software

The problem

The company knows everything.
Its website knows nothing.

  • Knowledge lives in the Wiki. Visitors get a contact form.
  • Leads wait. Someone types them into the CRM by hand.
  • Nobody sees which questions went unanswered.
Ambiguous clients already run Wiki, CRM, Calendar, Chat and Tasks in one workspace2

What we built

Concierge knows, acts and shows

Knows

Answers from the company Wiki, live, with sources. Says “I’ll check” instead of guessing.

Acts

Creates the deal, books the call, alerts sales, and turns unanswered questions into tasks.

Shows

Real interface instead of text walls: cards, a time picker, and it highlights the right plan on the page.

And the company steers it from one Wiki page. No code.

Generative UI with CopilotKit · data and actions through the Ambiguous API3
