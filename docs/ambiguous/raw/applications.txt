# Ambiguous applications

Use this index to choose an app for the user's workspace task. Each linked page also has a .md representation.

- [Docs](https://www.ambiguous.ai/applications/docs): Collaborative document editing for humans and AI coworkers.
- [Sheets](https://www.ambiguous.ai/applications/sheets): Organize and analyze tabular data. Your team writes formulas; your coworkers read ranges and patch cells.
- [Slides](https://www.ambiguous.ai/applications/slides): Presentation decks for humans and AI coworkers.
- [Wiki](https://www.ambiguous.ai/applications/wiki): Team knowledge base with nested pages, databases, and search for humans and AI coworkers.
- [Mail](https://www.ambiguous.ai/applications/mail): Email for humans and AI coworkers on workspace-owned domains.
- [Chat](https://www.ambiguous.ai/applications/chat): Real-time messaging for humans and AI coworkers.
- [Forms](https://www.ambiguous.ai/applications/forms): Surveys, questionnaires, and structured data collection for humans and AI coworkers.
- [Sign](https://www.ambiguous.ai/applications/sign): E-signature for humans and AI coworkers. ESIGN/UETA-compliant PKI seal on every contract.
- [Tasks](https://www.ambiguous.ai/applications/tasks): Task management with assignment to humans and AI coworkers.
- [Calendar](https://www.ambiguous.ai/applications/calendar): Scheduling, events, and availability for humans and AI coworkers.
- [CRM](https://www.ambiguous.ai/applications/crm): Contact and deal pipeline where every email, meeting, and doc auto-logs as activity, and your AI coworker moves deals forward.
- [Drive](https://www.ambiguous.ai/applications/drive): File storage and organization for humans and AI coworkers.
- [Identity](https://www.ambiguous.ai/applications/identity): Auth, workspace management, and access control for humans and AI coworkers. One user schema, one system, one front door.
- [Assistant](https://www.ambiguous.ai/applications/assistant): An AI teammate that plans, executes, and self-corrects across your workspace.
- [Admin](https://www.ambiguous.ai/applications/admin): Workspace management: member provisioning, billing dashboard, and audit log for humans and AI coworkers.
- [Automations](https://www.ambiguous.ai/applications/automations): Trigger, filter, and action workflows connecting webhooks, events, and the Assistant.
