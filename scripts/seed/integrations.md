Acme connects to the tools your fleet already uses. All integrations are included in every plan unless noted.

## ERP and finance

- **SAP S/4HANA**: two-way sync of work orders, parts and maintenance costs with SAP Plant Maintenance and Finance. Set up in about a day with our SAP connector. Growth and Enterprise plans.
- **QuickBooks Online**: push invoices and vendor bills.

## CRM and communication

- **Salesforce**: see fleet health and open work orders on the customer account.
- **Slack**: alerts for overdue services, failed inspections and parts running low.

## Telematics

- **Samsara**, **Geotab** and **Motive**: mileage and engine hours flow in automatically, so schedules stay accurate.

## API

A REST API and webhooks are available on all plans for anything not listed here.
